## What Kinemi is

Kinemi is film studio software. It turns what you describe into camera language, prompts and ComfyUI workflows, and sends them to compute you choose: ComfyUI on your own GPU, or your own Comfy Cloud account. **We don't render anything ourselves and we don't sell compute.** What a render costs at your provider is between you and them. Kinemi shows an estimate before every render; the actual cost can differ.

The chat runs on the assistant you pick: the built-in one, or a language model through your own OpenRouter account, or, on your own computer, the Claude Code or Codex you installed.
