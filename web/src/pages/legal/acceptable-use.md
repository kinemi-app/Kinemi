Kinemi can put a performance onto another face, build a cast of characters and make video that looks real. That is what makes it useful for film, and also why these rules exist. They apply to everything you do with Kinemi on this website, whatever compute renders it.

## Never

- **Sexual content involving minors.** Zero tolerance. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities.
- **Intimate or sexual imagery of real people without their consent,** including “nudified” photos and sexual deepfakes.
- **Deepfakes that deceive or harm:** putting real people into scenes they weren't in, or words in their mouths, to mislead, defraud, harass or damage their reputation.
- **Impersonation** of real people or organisations, for example to pass yourself off as them or to trick others.
- **Deception at scale:** fake news, election disinformation, fake reviews or evidence presented as real.
- **Harassment, threats and hate** against people or groups for who they are.
- **Glorifying or promoting violence, terrorism or violent extremism.**
- **Infringing other people's rights:** copyright, trademarks, likeness and privacy.
- **Anything illegal** where you are or where your content is shown.

## Real people and consent

Using a real person's face, body or voice, in Genjutsu, AI Cast or any other tool, needs their consent. For anyone famous, it also needs a legitimate purpose such as clearly labelled satire, commentary or criticism, and it must never pretend to be real.

## Label what's made with AI

If you publish video, images or audio that show real-looking people, places or events that didn't happen, say clearly that they were generated or changed with AI. In the EU this is a legal duty for deepfakes (EU AI Act, Art. 50). Keep the files' metadata intact where you can.

## The models' own rules

Every model has a license, some with use restrictions of their own: see [Licenses](/legal/licenses/). Breaking a model's license breaks these rules too.

## The service itself

- Don't try to get around limits or safety measures, or into other people's accounts or data.
- Don't attack or overload the service, and don't use it to spread malware.
- Don't use other people's keys or accounts without their permission.

## What happens

We may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these rules. Where it's appropriate, we warn first. Clearly illegal content, above all sexual content involving minors, we report to the authorities.

## Report abuse

Found something that breaks these rules? Write to {{abuseEmail}} with the link or a description. We look at every report.
